# The Boom of Bodybuilding Expos in India 2026: What to Expect

India's fitness expo circuit has genuinely expanded in 2026, though — worth flagging upfront — none of the major bodybuilding-focused expos land specifically in August or September. If you're planning around this scene, here's the real calendar and what each one actually offers.

## The major players in 2026

**IHFF (International Health, Sports & Fitness Festival)** runs twice this year: **June 12–14 in New Delhi** (Pragati Maidan) and **December 18–20 in Mumbai** (Bombay Exhibition Centre). It's one of the larger fitness-industry showcases, combining bodybuilding and physique contests with a broader trade-show floor — fitness equipment, nutrition brands, wellness tech, and a "GYMCON Business Lounge" aimed at gym-industry networking. The Mumbai edition has featured names like Phil Heath (7x Mr. Olympia) as a headline draw in past promotion.

**Sport India Expo / Fitness India Classic**, organized alongside the India International Sporting Goods Show, bundles a bodybuilding and powerlifting competition (Fitness India Classic — men's and women's physique, powerlifting) with a broader sporting-goods trade show, a "Sport India Run," and industry conferences. This one leans more B2B/trade-focused than fan-facing, useful if you're in the industry side of fitness rather than just competing or spectating.

**Musclemania India Tour** takes a different approach — rather than one big annual expo, it runs a rolling tour of natural bodybuilding shows across Indian cities through the year, including a Bengaluru stop and further stops later in 2026 (including one running into late October). Musclemania markets itself specifically as a natural/drug-tested alternative within India's bodybuilding scene, which is a notable differentiator if that matters to your audience.

**Extreme Pro** ran a bodybuilding and fitness expo at KTPO Hall in Bengaluru with walk-in registration from late May 2026 — a more regional, single-city event compared to IHFF's scale.

## What these expos actually offer athletes

- **Competition categories**: Most combine bodybuilding, men's and women's physique, and powerlifting into the same weekend, so if you're competing seriously, check which federation's rules a given expo follows before you commit to a prep cycle.
- **Industry access**: These are also where supplement brands, gym equipment manufacturers, and coaching services launch new products — useful if you're shopping for gear or services, less useful if you're purely there to watch.
- **Education and seminars**: IHFF in particular has leaned into wellness programming beyond pure bodybuilding — nutrition talks, mental health sessions, physiotherapy content — reflecting a broader shift in how these expos position themselves.

## Planning around the 2026 calendar

If you're building a training or travel plan around this circuit, the two dates worth locking in now are **IHFF Mumbai (Dec 18–20)** if you're targeting a year-end peak, or the Musclemania tour stops if you're pursuing natural bodybuilding specifically and want a lower-key regional show earlier in the year. For late-2026 planning, keep an eye on Musclemania's tour schedule, since city stops tend to get confirmed only a few months out.

Given how fast this calendar has grown in just the past year, it's worth checking each organizer's official site closer to your target date — new city stops and expo dates have been added mid-year more than once in 2026.
