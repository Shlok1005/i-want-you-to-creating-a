How it works

Four steps. One focused month.

The challenge follows Fitness Gurukul's proven story-map structure, tightened into a 4-week sprint with one coach guiding your training, nutrition, and accountability.

01

Baseline scan

We capture your current weight, routine, food habits, schedule, and goal so the coach knows exactly where to start.

02

Personal plan

You get a simple weekly training and nutrition direction built around your area, access, food preferences, and available time.

03

Train and track

Your coach reviews check-ins, adjusts targets, and keeps the momentum high through the full four weeks.

04

Result review

At the end, we review progress, celebrate wins, and map the next step so the change keeps going after the challenge.

FAQ

Quick answers before you join

What is the cost?

The challenge starts on 1 August. Submit the form or message on WhatsApp and the team will share the latest pricing and joining details.

Who is it for?

It is for people who want a focused reset: weight loss, better routine, improved strength, and practical nutrition guidance without overcomplicating the process.

What is included?

Baseline intake, 1 coach, weekly targets, training direction, Indian nutrition guidance, progress check-ins, accountability, and an end-of-challenge review.

What happens after 4 weeks?

You get a progress review and a recommended next step, whether that is a regular Fitness Gurukul plan, a maintenance routine, or another focused goal.
