How it works

Three months. One focused transformation.

The challenge runs fully online on the Fitness Gurukul App. Every participant tracks workouts and nutrition, updates measurements weekly, and builds a photo record from start to finish.

01

Register & baseline

Download the app, join the community WhatsApp group, and submit your registration — name, age, email, phone, front/side/back photos, weight, height, and body measurements.

02

Train on the app

Use the Fitness Gurukul App for the full 90 days. Track nutrition only on the app and log workouts daily — no exceptions.

03

Update & document

Weekly weight and body measurements are compulsory. Take one consistent daily photo, plus front/side/back photos every Sunday.

04

Share & finish strong

Post at least 4 stories and 1 reel each week with #FGTransformationChallenge and #FitnessGurukul. Missing updates for more than 5 consecutive days may lead to disqualification.

FAQ

Quick answers before you join

How long is the challenge?

90 days. Mode is online on the Fitness Gurukul App, and it is open for everyone.

How do I register?

Download the Fitness Gurukul App, join the Fitness Gurukul Community WhatsApp group, and fill the registration form with your details, photos (front/side/back), weight, height, and body measurements.

What are the app rules?

Every participant must use the Fitness Gurukul App throughout the challenge. Nutrition must be tracked exclusively on the app, and daily workout tracking is mandatory.

What about photos and updates?

Take one photo every day with the same light, place, camera, pose, and distance. Every Sunday take front, side, and back photos. Weekly weight and body measurement updates are compulsory. Missing updates for more than 5 consecutive days may lead to disqualification.

Are there social media requirements?

Post at least 4 stories and 1 reel (if possible) every week. Share workouts, meals, progress, or gym selfies with #FGTransformationChallenge and #FitnessGurukul.
